WHEREAS, Provider operates a software-as-a-service platform; and WHEREAS, Customer desires to engage Provider to provide such services subject to the terms set forth herein.

1. SERVICES. Provider shall provide the services described in one or more order forms executed by the parties (each, an "Order Form"). Each Order Form shall be incorporated into and form part of this Agreement upon execution.

2. FEES. Customer shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form. Unless otherwise specified, fees are payable within thirty (30) days of invoice date.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of twelve (12) months unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section 3.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party agrees to hold in confidence all non-public information disclosed by the other party and to use such information solely for the purposes of performing under this Agreement.
