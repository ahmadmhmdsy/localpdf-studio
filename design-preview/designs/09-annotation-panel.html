Service Agreement

This Service Agreement is entered into as of January 15, 2026 by and between Acme Corporation, a Delaware corporation, and the customer identified on the signature page.

WHEREAS, Provider operates a software-as-a-service platform; and WHEREAS, Customer desires to engage Provider to provide such services subject to the terms set forth herein.

1. SERVICES. Provider shall provide the services described in one or more order forms. Each Order Form shall be incorporated into and form part of this Agreement upon execution by both parties.

2. FEES. Customer shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form. Unless otherwise specified, fees are payable within thirty days of invoice date.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of twelve months unless earlier terminated.
